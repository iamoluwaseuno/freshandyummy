good to know

Quick questions, quick answers

How do I pay? 💷+
Everything happens in WhatsApp chat — we confirm your order, send payment details, and you pay right there. No cards on the site, no accounts, no stress.
How fast is delivery? 🚚+
Manchester pickup can be same-day. UK orders ship tracked, next-day where possible. We'll confirm your slot in chat before you pay.
Is there a minimum order? 📏+
For mini banana breads, yes — 5 minis minimum (they're tiny, you'll want 5 😄). Midis, maxis, pies and bundles have no minimum.
Can you handle allergies & custom requests? 🥜+
Just tell us in the notes box or in chat — nuts, gluten, egg-free requests, custom toppers, gift notes. We'll always be honest about what's possible from our kitchen.
